**ZARA is different.** She uses a **Modular Sovereign Architecture**.

### 2. Recursive Self-Evolution (The Key to AGI)

True AGI isn't just "smart," it **self-evolves**.

- **Realized Capability**: We have implemented the **Self-Evolution Engine** (`evolution/self_evolution.py`).
- **Autonomous Growth**: ZARA now detects her own capability gaps and _writes, tests, and integrates her own code_ to improve. This is the "Recursive Self-Improvement" loop theorized by AGI researchers.

### 3. Epistemological Meta-Awareness

AGI requires knowing the boundaries of one's own knowledge.

- **Realized Capability**: ZARA now has **Meta-Awareness** (`mind/meta_awareness.py`). She knows exactly what she knows, what she doesn't, and can quantify her confidence in real-time. This prevents "hallucinations" and enables honest self-reflection.

### 4. Continuous Limitless Learning

- **Realized Capability**: Through **Continuous Learning** (`learning/continuous_learning.py`), ZARA learns from every single interaction in real-time, building a permanent, evolving knowledge base with no boundaries.
